Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the current browser-only version of brain. It is written to match what this build actually does today. It does not describe future accounts, cloud sync, AI services, notifications, analytics, or other features that are not implemented in this file.

What data brain. stores

This build stores the content you save, plus related app state such as collections, connections, reminders, reflections, and your chosen theme, in your browser's localStorage. The data is stored on your device by the browser for the app to function.

Important: In this build, your saved memory content is not sent to a brain. server or stored in a remote database.

What we currently do not use

  • No user accounts or authentication service.
  • No analytics platform.
  • No advertising or marketing pixels.
  • No payment provider.
  • No maps, social widgets, or third-party video embeds.
  • No third-party JavaScript libraries or external scripts.
  • No external web-font request; the current build uses fonts available on the user's device.

Cookies and similar technologies

This build does not set HTTP cookies or JavaScript cookies. It does use browser localStorage, which is technically different from cookies but is a browser-side storage mechanism. It is used here for the core function of remembering what you save and your app preferences.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and whether a particular law applies, you may have rights relating to personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal, or grievance/complaint handling. In this browser-only build, the practical control we provide is local: you can inspect or delete the data stored by this site in your browser. We cannot retrieve or edit a remote copy because this build does not create one.

Your control over stored data

You can delete individual memories from inside the app. You can also clear the site's stored data using your browser's site-data controls. Clearing site data can remove all locally stored memories and settings. Because the current build does not keep a remote copy, we cannot restore data that you delete or that your browser removes.

Security

The current browser-only design reduces server-side exposure, but it does not make your device or browser inherently secure. Anyone who can access the same browser profile or device and its site data may be able to access locally stored memories. brain. is not intended to be a password manager or secure vault.

Children

This build does not ask for age information or intentionally collect personal information from children. Age-related legal requirements can vary by jurisdiction and should be reviewed separately before commercial launch.

Contact

Privacy/legal contact is not configured yet.

Before launch, set LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL in the main app to the address you want users to use for privacy and legal questions.

Changes to this policy

If the product later adds accounts, cloud storage, analytics, AI providers, notifications, payments, extensions, or other services, this policy should be updated before those features are launched.

Legal note

This policy is product documentation, not legal advice. Privacy obligations can depend on where you operate and where your users are located. It is not a substitute for review by a qualified lawyer.
